## Overview

**Epoch 4.0: Unleashing Tech Creativity and Innovation**

Epoch 4.0 is a premier GitHub Community Club event designed for tech enthusiasts, developers, and innovators. This event inspires creative expression with unique challenges and builds a strong tech community within GITAM University. By blending fun with learning, Epoch ensures a vibrant and engaging experience through games, performances, and tech exploration.

## Event Details

- **Dates:** 29th & 30th December, 2025
- **Venue:** GITAM University, Bengaluru Campus
- **Organized by:** GitHub Community at GITAM University & DoSL
- **Target Audience:** Students from all schools
- **Expected Participants:** 1000+ Students

## Background

Epoch, now in its fourth iteration, has evolved into a major tech fest hosted by the GitHub Community at GITAM University. The event offers a wide array of activities including technical workshops, gaming tournaments, and GeeksforGeeks coding contests.

## Objectives

- **Promoting Technology:** Encourage students to explore, learn, and innovate within various fields of technology
- **Teamwork and Collaboration:** Facilitate activities that require group participation, fostering teamwork among participants
- **Creative Expression:** Encourage students to think creatively through contests like videography and tech-based challenges
- **Building a Tech Community:** Strengthen the GitHub Community at GITAM as a hub for technical enthusiasts and learners
- **Enjoyment and Entertainment:** Blend learning with fun through engaging concepts like technology exploration, performances, and mini-stall games

## Epoch Coins System

The Epoch Coins System is a unified digital reward and activity currency designed to enhance student engagement throughout Epoch 4.0.

### What Are Epoch Coins?

Epoch Coins are the official virtual currency of Epoch 4.0. They act as participation credits that students can use across multiple entertainment and experience-based zones.

### How to Use Coins

Participants can redeem Epoch Coins at:
- **Game Zones** – Arcade games, mini challenges, competitive activities
- **VR Booths** – Immersive VR experiences and interactive simulations
- **Fun & Activity Zones** – Any experience-based stall supporting the coins system

### Earning More Coins

1. **Recharge Stations**
     - Special recharge counters placed across the venue
     - Earn additional coins by playing selected mini-games or completing quick challenges

2. **Attend Tech Workshops & Sessions**
     - Participating in approved workshops grants bonus coins
     - Promotes learning while keeping the fest experience gamified

3. **Event Participation Bonuses**
     - Students participating in flagship competitions may earn additional coins

### Purpose & Benefits

- Encourages students to explore the entire fest
- Incentivizes participation in learning-based activities
- Ensures fair and controlled usage of resources in busy zones
- Provides a gamified and enjoyable experience for attendees
